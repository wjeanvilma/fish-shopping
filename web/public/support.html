Support

Fish Shopping help · saves, unlocks, and common issues.

First session

Fish → cut → stock → sell. Coaching advances on state events, not animation timing.

Saves & recovery

  • Local slots keep a latest and previous backup.
  • If latest is corrupt, choose Recover previous on the title screen.
  • Private / blocked browser storage falls back to memory-only — export is not durable until storage works.

Purchases

Use Restore purchases in Goals after reinstalling. The one-time harbor unlock and cosmetics are power-neutral for cosmetics and idempotent for the unlock grant.

Performance

Lower Quality in Settings if frames stutter. The simulation stays fixed at 20 Hz; quality only changes presentation cost.
