Privacy Policy

Fish Shopping · published for store legal URLs; Play Data safety answers live in shared/store/data-safety.json (Phase 8).

Fish Shopping is a local-first harbor management game. You can play entirely offline. Optional features only run after you choose them.

What we store on your device

  • Game saves (latest + previous backup) in app private storage or browser IndexedDB.
  • Presentation settings (audio, quality, accessibility, control preferences).
  • Optional entitlement records so Restore purchases can recover a one-time unlock / cosmetics.

Optional cloud sync

If you turn Cloud sync on and Firebase keys are configured, an anonymous account may link a checksummed save snapshot. We do not require an email or display name for that path. You can leave Cloud sync off and keep playing locally.

Optional analytics

Analytics are off until you opt in (title gate or Settings). When enabled, only allowlisted events are considered: tutorial steps, first sale, harbor unlocked, offline claimed, save recovery, and frame-time summaries. We do not send account ids, positions, bag contents, or full saves.

Purchases

In-app purchases (full harbor unlock and power-neutral cosmetics) are processed by the platform store (Google Play or the web checkout API). We do not receive your full payment card details.
